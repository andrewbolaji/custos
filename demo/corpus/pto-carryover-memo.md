# HR Policy Update: PTO Carryover Change for 2026

**From:** Priya Chandrasekaran, VP of People
**Date:** November 18, 2025
**Effective:** January 1, 2026
**Distribution:** All Larkspur Analytics employees

## Summary

Effective January 1, 2026, Larkspur Analytics is moving to a **use-it or
lose-it PTO policy with no carryover**. This memo supersedes the PTO
carryover language in the Employee Handbook, which has not yet been
reissued to reflect this change.

## What is changing

Under the policy described in the Employee Handbook, employees could carry
over up to 10 unused PTO days into the following calendar year, to be used
by March 31. **That carryover allowance ends as of this policy.** Starting
with the 2026 calendar year:

- All PTO accrued in a calendar year must be used by December 31 of that
  year.
- Any unused, accrued PTO remaining on December 31 is forfeited. It is not
  paid out and does not carry into January.
- This does not change accrual rates, which remain 1.67 days per month (20
  days per year) as described in the handbook.
- State-mandated exceptions still apply. Employees in states where PTO
  carryover or payout is legally required (currently: California, Illinois,
  Montana) are unaffected by this change and should contact People Ops for
  their state-specific policy.

## Why we are making this change

Outside counsel flagged that our prior carryover cap, combined with how we
accrue PTO as "earned wages" in several states, created wage-and-hour
exposure that a strict use-it-or-lose-it policy avoids in states where it is
permitted. This is a compliance-driven change, not a benefits reduction —
the accrual rate is unchanged, and we are giving six weeks' notice before
the 2025 year-end so nobody is caught off guard by the December 31 cutoff.

## What you should do

If you are carrying a PTO balance you were expecting to use in Q1 2026
under the old carryover rule, plan to use it by December 31, 2025 instead.
Managers have been asked to approve reasonable year-end PTO requests
generously given the short notice.

## Questions

Route questions to your manager first, then to People Ops at
peopleops@larkspur-example.com if your manager cannot answer them. This memo
is the authoritative statement of the 2026 PTO carryover policy; the
Employee Handbook's PTO section will be corrected in its next scheduled
revision, but until then, this memo governs.
