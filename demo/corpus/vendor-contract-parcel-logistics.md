# Vendor Agreement: Parcel Logistics Partners

**Classification:** Restricted — Finance access only.
**Parties:** Larkspur Analytics, Inc. ("Customer") and Parcel Logistics
Partners LLC ("Vendor")
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Contract Term:** 12 months, does not auto-renew

## Services

Vendor handles equipment fulfillment logistics: shipping and receiving for
company laptops and peripherals to new hires and returning equipment from
departing employees, per the process described in the New Hire Onboarding
Runbook and the IT Equipment & Acceptable Use Policy.

## Fees

- Per-shipment fee: $22 domestic, $58 international, plus carrier cost
  passed through at cost.
- Monthly minimum: none. Larkspur is billed only for shipments actually
  used.
- Invoiced monthly in arrears, net-30.

## Term & renewal

This agreement has a fixed 12-month term from the Effective Date and **does
not auto-renew**. To continue service beyond the term, the parties must
execute a new agreement or a written renewal amendment before the term
expires. If no renewal is executed, the agreement simply ends on the term
expiration date with no further action required by either party.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience, at any time
during the term, with **30 days' written notice** — the shortest notice
period of Larkspur's current vendor agreements. Termination for cause
(material breach, uncured within 15 days of written notice) is available
immediately without the 30-day notice period.

## Data handling on termination

Vendor's access is limited to shipping addresses and equipment serial
numbers, provided per-shipment via a scoped API integration; Vendor does
not retain a standing copy of Larkspur's employee or contractor address
book. On termination, Vendor confirms deletion of any cached shipment
records older than the current 90-day operational window within 10
business days.

## Service level

Vendor commits to shipment initiation within 1 business day of a request
and provides tracking numbers within 24 hours of carrier pickup. No formal
uptime SLA applies, as this is a fulfillment service rather than a hosted
platform.

## Point of contact

Vendor operations contact: ops@parcellogistics.example.org. Larkspur's
contract owner is Finance; day-to-day shipment requests route through IT,
per the onboarding and offboarding checklists.
