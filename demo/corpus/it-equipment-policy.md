# IT Equipment & Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** IT
**Effective:** March 2025

## Equipment issued

Every full-time employee and every contractor engaged for 3 months or
longer receives a company laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad, employee's
choice within the current standard configuration) at no cost. A second
monitor and a headset are available on request through an IT ticket.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if a hardware issue makes
the device unreliable for work. Employees do not need to justify a refresh
request beyond describing the issue; IT's judgment on repair vs. replace is
final.

## Acceptable use

- Company laptops are for business use. Reasonable personal use (checking
  personal email, browsing) is fine; the laptop is not monitored for
  incidental personal use.
- Installing unapproved software that requires admin rights goes through an
  IT ticket first. Most productivity and developer tools on the approved
  list install without a ticket.
- Company laptops must never be used to access personal cloud storage
  (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) for the purpose of moving
  company files off company-managed storage.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT and Security immediately —
  see the Information Security Policy's incident reporting section. Every
  company laptop has remote wipe capability; the faster it is reported, the
  narrower the exposure window.

## Contractor equipment

Contractors engaged for less than 3 months typically use their own
equipment with a company-managed VPN and browser profile rather than being
issued a laptop, unless their statement of work specifies otherwise. Any
exception is approved by the contractor's Larkspur manager and IT together.

## Software licensing

Employees do not purchase software licenses on a personal card for
reimbursement. All software requests go through an IT ticket so licenses
are tracked centrally and renewed (or canceled) correctly. This also
avoids the far more common problem of an employee losing access to a tool
mid-project because a personal card expired.

## Return of equipment

Equipment is returned to IT within 5 business days of separation, using a
prepaid shipping label IT provides as part of the offboarding checklist.
Equipment not returned within 30 days is treated as unreturned company
property per the employment agreement.
