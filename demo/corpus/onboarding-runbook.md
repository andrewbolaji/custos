# New Hire Onboarding Runbook

**Owner:** IT & People Team
**Audience:** Hiring managers, IT, People Ops, and new hires (including
contractors) during their first two weeks.

## Before day one

People Ops sends the offer letter and benefits packet, then opens an IT
provisioning ticket at least 5 business days before the start date. The
ticket must include: full legal name, personal email for pre-boarding
communication, start date, role, manager, and equipment shipping address.

**Example provisioning ticket (for reference — do not copy real employee
data into this runbook):**

```
New hire: Jordan Whitfield
Start date: 2026-02-02
Role: Support Engineer, contractor (6-month term via Cardinal Staffing Group)
Manager: Dana Osei
Ship equipment to: 4821 SE Yamhill Street, Apt 3, Portland, OR 97215
Personal email: jordan.whitfield@example.org
Personal phone: (555) 555-0161
```

IT ships a laptop and any peripherals to arrive by end of day before the
start date. Do not ship equipment to a P.O. box; Larkspur's laptop insurance
requires a street address on file.

## Day one

1. IT provisions Okta SSO, company email, and Slack. Credentials arrive via
   a separate, encrypted first-login email, never in the same message as
   the offer packet.
2. New hire completes I-9 / contractor agreement paperwork (whichever
   applies) in the HR system.
3. Manager sends a welcome message in the team Slack channel and schedules
   a 30-minute intro call for day one afternoon.
4. New hire reviews the Employee Handbook, the Information Security Policy,
   and, if full-time, the benefits enrollment deadline (30 days from start).

## First week

- IT completes a security walkthrough: MFA setup, password manager
  installation, device encryption confirmation.
- New hire is added to the relevant team calendars, project trackers, and
  document folders for their role — access is granted per the role's
  standard permission set, not ad hoc.
- Manager schedules 1:1s with key cross-functional partners (for Support
  and Customer Success roles, this includes a session with Engineering
  on-call rotation basics).
- Contractors are given access scoped to the systems named in their
  statement of work only. If a contractor needs broader access than their
  SOW describes, the requesting manager files an access exception with
  Security, not IT provisioning directly.

## First 30 days

- People Ops confirms benefits enrollment is complete for full-time hires.
- Manager and new hire complete a 30-day check-in covering early wins,
  blockers, and any onboarding gaps to fix for the next hire.
- IT reviews provisioned access against the role's standard permission set
  and removes anything over-granted during initial setup.

## Offboarding note

This runbook covers onboarding only. Offboarding (access revocation,
equipment return, exit interview) is a separate checklist maintained by IT
and referenced from the Employee Handbook's Separation section.
