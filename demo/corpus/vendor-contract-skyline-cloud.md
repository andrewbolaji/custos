# Vendor Agreement: Skyline Cloud Hosting

**Classification:** Restricted — Finance access only.
**Parties:** Larkspur Analytics, Inc. ("Customer") and Skyline Cloud
Hosting, Inc. ("Vendor")
**Effective Date:** 2024-08-01
**Contract Term:** 24 months, auto-renewing (see Term & Renewal)

## Services

Vendor provides primary cloud infrastructure hosting for Larkspur's
production data warehouse and analytics processing pipeline, including
compute, block storage, and managed networking in the us-east-1 and
us-west-2 regions.

## Fees

- Base platform fee: $18,500/month, covering reserved compute capacity.
- Usage-based overage: metered monthly at Vendor's published rate card,
  invoiced net-30.
- Annual fee review: Vendor may propose a rate change with 90 days' notice
  before the renewal date; Customer may terminate rather than accept an
  increase greater than 8% year-over-year (see Termination).

## Term & renewal

This agreement has an initial term of 24 months from the Effective Date.
Following the initial term, it **auto-renews for successive 12-month
terms** unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least
**60 days before** the then-current term's expiration.

## Termination

- **For convenience:** Either party may terminate this agreement, including
  during an active renewal term, with **90 days' written notice**.
- **For cause:** Either party may terminate immediately on written notice
  if the other party materially breaches this agreement and fails to cure
  within 30 days of written notice describing the breach.
- **For a fee increase above the 8% cap:** Customer may terminate with 30
  days' notice, without the standard 90-day notice period, if Vendor
  proposes a renewal rate increase exceeding 8% year-over-year and the
  parties do not agree on a lower rate within 30 days of the proposal.

## Data handling on termination

Vendor provides a full export of Customer's data within 15 days of
termination taking effect, in a format specified in the Data Processing
Addendum (Exhibit B, not reproduced in this summary). Vendor permanently
deletes Customer data from its systems within 30 days after the export is
confirmed received, except where retention is required by law.

## Service level

99.95% monthly uptime commitment on the primary compute and storage
services, with service credits per the SLA schedule in Exhibit A for any
month falling below that threshold. SLA credits are Customer's sole
remedy for uptime shortfalls; they do not affect the termination rights
above.

## Point of contact

Vendor account management: accounts@skylinecloud.example.com. Larkspur's
contract owner is Finance; operational/technical issues route through the
Platform team, not this contract's Finance point of contact.
