# Travel & Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance
**Effective:** June 2025
**Applies to:** All employees. Contractors follow the expense terms in
their individual agreement instead of this policy.

## Overview

This policy covers what Larkspur reimburses, how to submit expenses, and
the approval chain. When in doubt about whether something is reimbursable,
ask before you spend, not after.

## Booking travel

Flights and hotels for company travel are booked through the company travel
portal (Navan). Book at least 14 days in advance when possible; last-minute
bookings need a one-line justification in the expense report.

- **Flights:** Economy for flights under 5 hours. Premium economy is
  reimbursable for flights over 5 hours with manager pre-approval.
- **Hotels:** Up to $275/night in most US cities, up to $350/night in New
  York, San Francisco, and Boston. Book through the portal so the corporate
  rate applies automatically.
- **Ground transportation:** Rideshare, taxi, or rental car are all fine.
  Choose whichever is cheapest for the trip; keep receipts for anything
  over $25.

## Meals

Per diem for company travel is $75/day, covering breakfast, lunch, and
dinner, with no receipts required under the per diem cap. Team meals
(client dinners, offsite team dinners) are booked separately and require
the attendee list in the expense note.

## Submitting expenses

All expenses are submitted through Expensify within 30 days of the
purchase. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after purchase require
Finance director approval and may not be reimbursed. Attach a receipt for
any single expense over $25.

Approval chain:

1. Direct manager approves expenses under $500.
2. Department head approves expenses $500–$2,000.
3. CFO approves anything over $2,000, or any expense outside this policy's
   normal categories.

## What is not reimbursable

- Airline seat upgrades beyond premium economy.
- Alcohol on solo trips (client entertainment is handled differently — see
  below).
- Personal entertainment, including in-room movies and minibar charges.
- Traffic or parking violations.
- Spouse, partner, or family travel costs, even when traveling alongside
  company business.

## Client entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable with a business purpose
noted (who attended, what was discussed) and are not subject to the $75/day
per diem cap, but should stay reasonable for the context. Anything over
$300 for a single client event needs department head pre-approval.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than once a quarter are issued a corporate card
on request. The card is for business expenses only; personal charges on
the corporate card, even if reimbursed immediately, are a policy violation
and repeated instances are handled through the process in the Code of
Conduct document.
