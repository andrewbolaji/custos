# Code of Conduct & Workplace Standards

**Owner:** People Team
**Effective:** January 2024
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and interns.

## Our standard

Larkspur Analytics expects every person representing the company —
employee, contractor, or intern — to treat colleagues, customers, and
partners with respect. This document describes what that means concretely
and what happens when it is not met.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate directly and respectfully, including in disagreement.
- Give credit for others' work and ideas.
- Respect boundaries around working hours, time off, and personal space,
  including in remote and hybrid settings.
- Escalate conflicts through a manager or People Ops rather than letting
  them fester or spill into shared channels.

## Prohibited behavior

- Harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation,
  religion, age, disability, national origin, or any other legally
  protected characteristic.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Falsifying expense reports, timesheets, or other company records.
- Sharing restricted or internal data (see the Information Security
  Policy's data classification) outside the company without authorization.
- Using company systems, including any AI assistant with access to company
  data, to attempt to bypass an access control or approval step that would
  otherwise apply to the same action taken manually.

## Reporting a concern

Reports can be made to a manager, to People Ops directly at
peopleops@larkspur-example.com, or, for concerns involving a manager or
People Ops staff, to the anonymous ethics line at (555) 555-0199. Reports
made in good faith are protected from retaliation regardless of the
investigation's outcome.

## Investigation process

People Ops reviews every report within 5 business days and determines
whether a formal investigation is warranted. Both the reporting party and
the subject of a report are kept informed of process (not necessarily
outcome details, which may be confidential) at each stage.

## Consequences

Consequences scale with severity and pattern, from a documented coaching
conversation up to immediate termination for serious violations
(harassment, data misuse, falsification of records). A first minor
infraction typically results in coaching and documentation; a pattern of
infractions or a single serious one moves directly to formal disciplinary
action, which may include termination.

## Contractor-specific note

Contractors are held to the same conduct standard as employees while
engaged with Larkspur. A conduct violation by a contractor is handled
jointly with the contractor's staffing agency or employer of record, and
may result in the engagement being ended immediately regardless of the
remaining contract term.

## Questions

If something in this document is unclear, or you are unsure whether a
situation rises to the level of a formal report, People Ops would rather
answer an unnecessary question than have a real concern go unreported.
