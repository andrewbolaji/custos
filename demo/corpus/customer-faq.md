# Customer Support FAQ

**Owner:** Customer Success
**Audience:** External customers; also used internally by Support as a
reference for answering common questions consistently.

## Account and billing

**How do I upgrade my plan?**
From the account settings page, select Plan, then Upgrade. Upgrades take
effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the current
billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing
cycle.

**When is my card charged?**
Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month as your
original signup date. Annual plans are charged once per year on the
signup anniversary. If a charge fails, we retry after 3 days and again
after 7 days before pausing the account.

**Can I get an invoice instead of a card charge?**
Annual plans on the Growth tier or above can request invoice billing net-30
by contacting billing@larkspur-example.com. Monthly plans are card-only.

**How do I cancel?**
Cancel from account settings, or email support@larkspur-example.com.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; we do
not offer partial-period refunds except where required by local law.

## Data and integrations

**What data sources does Larkspur connect to?**
Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Klaviyo out of the box.
Custom data sources can be connected via our CSV import or the public API.

**How often does data sync?**
Ad platform and e-commerce data syncs every 4 hours by default. Growth
tier and above can enable hourly sync from account settings.

**Where is my data stored, and for how long?**
Customer data is stored in AWS us-east-1. Raw synced data is retained for
13 months; aggregated reporting data is retained for the life of the
account. Deleting your account removes raw data within 30 days and
aggregated data within 90 days.

## Support

**What are support hours?**
Chat and email support are available Monday–Friday, 6 AM–6 PM Pacific.
Growth tier and above also get a dedicated Slack Connect channel with their
Customer Success contact.

**How do I report a bug?**
Email support@larkspur-example.com with what you were trying to do, what
happened instead, and a screenshot if possible. Include your account email
so we can look up your workspace quickly.

**Do you have an SLA?**
Enterprise tier includes a formal SLA (99.9% uptime, response time
commitments) detailed in the Enterprise contract. Other tiers are best
effort; our actual median first-response time over the last quarter was
under 2 hours during support hours.

## Security

**Is my data shared with other customers?**
No. Each customer's data is logically isolated in our data warehouse and
access-controlled per account. We do not use one customer's data to train
models or generate insights for another customer.

**Do you have a security questionnaire we can review?**
Yes — contact your Customer Success representative or
security@larkspur-example.com and we will share our current security
documentation under NDA if needed.
