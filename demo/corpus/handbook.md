# Larkspur Analytics — Employee Handbook

**Effective:** January 2024
**Applies to:** All Larkspur Analytics employees and, where noted, contractors.
**Owner:** People Team (people@larkspur-example.com)

Welcome to Larkspur Analytics. This handbook is the starting point for how we
work together. It is not a contract of employment, and specific policies
(security, expenses, remote work) are maintained as their own documents —
this handbook links to them rather than duplicating them, so always check the
standalone policy if this handbook and another document seem to disagree on
a detail.

## About Larkspur

Larkspur Analytics builds marketing attribution software for mid-market
e-commerce brands. We were founded in 2018 and are headquartered in
Portland, Oregon, with a fully remote-eligible engineering org. As of this
handbook's effective date we have approximately 140 employees across
Product, Engineering, Customer Success, Sales, Finance, and People.

## Employment basics

All employees are at-will unless a signed offer letter states otherwise.
Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM in the employee's local time
zone, with core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific expected
for cross-team meetings.

New hires complete onboarding under the guidance of the **New Hire
Onboarding Runbook**, which covers IT provisioning, benefits enrollment
deadlines, and first-week expectations.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.67 days per month (20 days per
year), starting from their first day of employment. PTO is requested through
the HR system and requires manager approval at least 5 business days in
advance for any request longer than 2 consecutive days.

**Carryover.** Employees may carry over up to 10 unused PTO days into the
following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the
new year or they are forfeited. This carryover allowance has been in place
since the policy was first published and is the standard most managers
quote when asked about year-end PTO planning.

Unused PTO beyond the carryover cap is not paid out except where required by
state law at termination.

## Holidays

Larkspur observes 10 paid company holidays per year, published each
December for the following year on the internal People Team wiki. Employees
in states with additional legally mandated holidays are covered per local
law.

## Benefits

Full-time employees are eligible for the following, effective the first of
the month following 30 days of employment:

- Medical, dental, and vision insurance (Larkspur covers 80% of the employee
  premium and 60% of dependent premiums).
- 401(k) with a 4% company match, fully vested after 1 year.
- $75/month remote work stipend (see the Remote & Hybrid Work Policy for
  what this covers).
- An annual $1,200 learning and development budget, usable for courses,
  conferences, or books relevant to the employee's role.

Questions about benefits enrollment go to benefits@larkspur-example.com or
the People Team's front desk line at (555) 555-0142.

## Performance and growth

Formal performance conversations happen twice a year, in June and December.
Compensation changes tied to performance reviews are communicated by a
manager and documented in the HR system; band structure itself is
maintained separately by Finance and is not part of this handbook.

## Workplace conduct

All employees are expected to follow the **Code of Conduct & Workplace
Standards** document, which covers anti-harassment policy, reporting
channels, and disciplinary process in full. This handbook does not restate
it.

## Separation

Employees who resign are asked to give at least 2 weeks' notice. Final pay,
including any PTO payout required by state law, is processed on the next
regular payroll cycle following the last day worked. IT access is
deactivated on the last day of employment per the offboarding checklist
maintained by IT.

## Questions

If anything in this handbook is unclear, or seems to conflict with a more
specific policy document, ask the People Team before assuming either
document is wrong — policies are occasionally updated by memo before this
handbook's next scheduled revision catches up.
