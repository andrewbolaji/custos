# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Owner:** People Team
**Effective:** September 2024

## Eligibility

Larkspur Analytics is remote-eligible for most roles. Engineering, Product,
Customer Success, and Support roles are fully remote-eligible by default.
Sales roles based near a customer hub (currently Portland and Austin) are
expected in a co-working space 2 days a week for in-person customer
meetings; this is noted in the offer letter when it applies.

## Home office stipend

Employees working remotely more than 3 days a week receive a one-time
$500 home office setup stipend in their first 90 days, plus the $75/month
ongoing remote work stipend described in the Employee Handbook's Benefits
section. The ongoing stipend covers home internet and a portion of
utilities; it is paid through payroll, not reimbursed through Expensify.

## Core hours and availability

Regardless of time zone, employees are expected to be reachable during core
collaboration hours: 10:00 AM–3:00 PM Pacific. Outside of core hours,
employees manage their own schedule, provided deliverables and meeting
commitments are met.

## Equipment

Company-issued laptops, monitors (on request), and a headset are shipped to
the employee's home address on file, coordinated through the New Hire
Onboarding Runbook for new hires and through an IT ticket for existing
employees. Equipment remains company property and is returned at
separation per the offboarding checklist.

## In-person expectations

Fully remote employees are expected to attend two in-person events per
year: the spring all-company offsite and one team-specific gathering,
travel costs covered per the Travel & Expense Policy. Attendance
exceptions are handled by the employee's manager on a case-by-case basis.

## Time zone coverage

Teams with customer-facing coverage requirements (Support, Customer
Success) publish their own coverage schedule separately; this policy does
not set specific shift hours, only the general core-hours expectation
above.

## Security note

Remote work does not change the requirements in the Information Security
Policy: full-disk encryption, MFA, and no accessing the production console
or customer data warehouse from a personal device apply identically
whether an employee works from an office or from home.
