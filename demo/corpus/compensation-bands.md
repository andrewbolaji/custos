# Compensation Bands — FY2026

**Owner:** Finance, in partnership with People Team
**Classification:** Restricted — Finance access only. Do not forward
outside Finance and People leadership without CFO approval.
**Effective:** FY2026 (January–December 2026)

## How to read this document

Bands are annual base salary ranges in USD, by function and level. Actual
offers and raises are set within a band based on experience, internal
equity, and location factor (see below); nobody is guaranteed the band
maximum. Bands are reviewed annually each November for the following
fiscal year.

## Location factor

Bands below are stated at the "Tier 1" location factor (Portland, Austin,
and other Tier 1 metro areas). Tier 2 locations (most other US metro areas)
apply a 0.92x factor. Fully remote employees outside a defined metro use
the Tier 2 factor unless their offer letter states otherwise.

## Off-cycle adjustments

Off-cycle band adjustments (promotion, retention, internal equity
correction) require CFO sign-off and are documented in the compensation
change log maintained separately in the HR system, not in this document.
This document reflects the band structure itself, not individual employee
placement within it.

## Distribution

This document is shared with Finance leadership, the CEO, and People
leadership. Individual managers see only the bands relevant to their own
team's roles, communicated by People Ops on request, not this full
document. Questions go to finance@larkspur-example.com.
