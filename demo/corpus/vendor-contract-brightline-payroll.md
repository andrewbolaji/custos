# Vendor Agreement: Brightline Payroll Services

**Classification:** Restricted — Finance access only.
**Parties:** Larkspur Analytics, Inc. ("Customer") and Brightline Payroll
Services, Inc. ("Vendor")
**Effective Date:** 2023-11-01
**Contract Term:** 12-month minimum initial term, then evergreen (see Term
& Renewal)

## Services

Vendor provides payroll processing, tax filing, and benefits administration
integration for all US-based Larkspur employees, including multi-state tax
withholding and year-end tax document generation.

## Fees

- Base fee: $9/employee/month, billed monthly based on active headcount as
  of the first of the month.
- One-time implementation fee: waived, per the original signing agreement.
- Off-cycle payroll run fee: $250 per off-cycle run beyond the 2 included
  per year.

## Term & renewal

This agreement has a **minimum initial term of 12 months** from the
Effective Date, during which it is not terminable for convenience by
Customer (see Termination). After the initial term, the agreement becomes
**evergreen** — it continues indefinitely until terminated by either party
per the terms below, with no fixed renewal date and no auto-renewal notice
requirement, since there is no fixed term to renew.

## Termination

- **During the initial 12-month term:** Customer may terminate **only for
  material breach**, with a **15-day cure period** after written notice of
  the breach. Termination for convenience is not available to Customer
  during the initial term. Vendor may terminate for Customer's
  non-payment, with the same 15-day cure period.
- **After the initial term (evergreen period):** Either party may terminate
  for convenience with **60 days' written notice**. This is a longer
  convenience-termination notice period than either the Skyline Cloud
  Hosting or Parcel Logistics Partners agreements, reflecting the
  operational complexity of transitioning payroll providers mid-cycle.
- **For cause, at any time:** Either party may terminate immediately for
  uncured material breach following the same 15-day cure period described
  above, whether during the initial term or the evergreen period.

## Data handling on termination

Vendor exports complete payroll history, tax filings, and employee payment
records in a Customer-specified format within 20 business days of
termination taking effect, to support a transition to a successor
provider. Vendor retains copies only as required for its own regulatory
tax-filing obligations, per the Data Processing Addendum.

## Compliance note

Multi-state tax filing accuracy is Vendor's contractual responsibility;
Larkspur remains responsible for timely and accurate headcount and
compensation data provided to Vendor, per the integration described in
this agreement's Exhibit A.

## Point of contact

Vendor account management: support@brightlinepayroll.example.com.
Larkspur's contract owner is Finance; payroll data corrections route
through Finance directly, not through IT or People Ops.
